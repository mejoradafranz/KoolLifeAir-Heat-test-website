YOUR PRIVACY
MATTERS TO US

This policy explains what information Kool Life Air & Heat collects through this website and how we use it. Effective October 30, 2026.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. When you fill out a quote, contact, scheduling, or free estimate form, we receive the details you enter, such as:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Your service address and the service you need
  • Any message or notes you include

If you call or email us, we receive your phone number or email address and anything you tell us.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your request, schedule and perform service, send estimates and invoices, and follow up about your HVAC system. We may contact you about your maintenance plan or service reminders if you are an existing customer.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when needed to run our business:

  • Form processing: our website forms are delivered to us through Formspree, a third-party form service.
  • Financing: if you apply for financing, your application is handled by the financing provider under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law or to protect our rights.

Third-Party Services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those providers may receive basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type. Links to Facebook, Instagram, Google, or other sites are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can ask us to update or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, at any time by emailing koollifeairheat@gmail.com or calling (689) 378-0460.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
